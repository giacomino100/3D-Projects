Descrição

Bring the true spirit of Christmas into your home with this beautiful Minimalist Nativity Scene!

Featuring Mary holding Baby Jesus and Joseph with his shepherd's staff, this sculpture combines simplicity, elegance, and the timeless meaning of Christmas.

Designed with smooth curves, faceless figures, and soft organic shapes, this piece creates a modern and peaceful interpretation of the Holy Family.

✨ Highlights:

  • Minimalist and elegant design
  • Beautiful in a single filament color
  • Smooth, rounded sculptural shapes
  • Perfect for Christmas home decorations
  • A meaningful handmade gift for family and friends
  • Ideal for shelves, tables, and holiday displays

🎄 Suggested filament colors: Ivory, matte white, beige, or wood-tone PLA.
